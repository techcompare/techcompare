How it works

A worked example

Say a crossword gives you four squares and you know the 2nd and 4th letters are A and E. The theme points to an R, and you’ve ruled out T.

  1. 1Set the pattern to ?a?e.
  2. 2Put r in Must include.
  3. 3Put t in Exclude.
  4. 4Scan the shortlist: bare, care, dare, fare, rage, rake, rare…

Tips & common mistakes

  • The pattern sets the length. ?a?e only matches four-letter words, so leave the pattern blank if you’re not sure how long the word is.
  • Repeated letters count. Typing ee in Must include asks for at least two E’s.
  • Positions start at 1. 1=s is the first letter. Separate entries with commas or spaces.
  • Grey isn’t always absent. In Wordle, a grey tile on a repeated letter means there are no more copies of it. The helper accounts for that.
  • Use hint mode. It shows only the first letter of each result, so you can get a nudge without seeing the answer.

Quick guides for stuck moments

A few reliable habits can turn a long shortlist into the answer you were looking for.

?

Crossword clues

Start with the checked letters, not the clue alone. A pattern such as ?a?e gives you the word length and lets the finder surface plausible spellings quickly.

  • Enter every confirmed letter in its exact position.
  • Use Must include for a themed or clued letter.
  • Keep an open mind about plurals and verb forms.
★

Scrabble racks

Look for a strong base word first, then check whether a short prefix or suffix lets you use an awkward tile. Blank tiles are most valuable when they complete a high-scoring hook.

  • Try the rack with one blank represented by ?.
  • Sort by score when you need the biggest play.
  • Check two-letter words before settling for a seven-letter word.
+

Wordle strategy

Use an opener with common, distinct letters, then turn each colour into a constraint. The helper keeps track of repeated letters so you can focus on the next useful guess.

  • Green tiles lock a letter into place.
  • Yellow tiles belong in the word, but not there.
  • Grey tiles eliminate letters unless another copy is confirmed.

Questions

Frequently asked

Which dictionary do you use?

The ENABLE word list, a public-domain list of about 172,000 English words used by many word games. It includes plurals and verb forms, and leaves out proper nouns and abbreviations.

Is my search sent anywhere?

No. The dictionary is downloaded once and every search runs in your browser. Nothing you type leaves your device.

Why is a word missing, or why does a strange word appear?

Each game uses its own list. Wordle accepts fewer answers than it accepts guesses, and crosswords allow phrases and names. Check a result against the game you’re playing.

How are Scrabble scores calculated?

Each word gets the standard English tile values, with no board bonuses. In Scrabble Rack mode, a letter covered by a blank tile scores zero.

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no accounts, no paywalls and no limits on how many searches you run.
